Patterns & principles

06
Principle

Crimson means action.

Brand red appears on primary actions, agent presence, and approval flow — never on decoration. If everything is red, nothing is.

Principle

Auditable by default.

Every action shows its source, cost, and trail. IDs and timestamps in mono so they're scannable and copyable.

Principle

Built mobile-first.

Approve on the train, configure on the desk. 390px is the design floor; every screen scales up from there.
